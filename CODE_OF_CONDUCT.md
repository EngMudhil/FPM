# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Funded Portfolio Manager project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints
* Accepting constructive feedback
* Focusing on what is best for the project

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

* Harassment, trolling, or insulting comments
* Publishing others’ private information
* Conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards. Instances of abusive or unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers.
